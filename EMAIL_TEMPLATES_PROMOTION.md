## Tips for Success

1. **Personalize every email** - mention specific content they created
2. **Lead with value** - what's in it for THEM, not you
3. **Be patient** - responses take 1-2 weeks
4. **Follow up once** - then move on
5. **Track everything** - use the sheet above

## Best Times to Send

- **Tuesday-Thursday**: 9-11am
- **Avoid**: Mondays (inbox full), Fridays (checked out)
- **Best months**: January (planning season), March (spring prep)
