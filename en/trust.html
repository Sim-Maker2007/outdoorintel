Reference Standard

Trust & Sources

Outdoor Intel is being rebuilt around sourced, Canadian outdoor planning data. A page is only as useful as its freshness, source quality, and correction path.

Verification Labels

  • Needs review: a generated or imported profile has source links, but the practical details still need editorial verification.
  • Verified: the page has been checked against official sources and local planning details, with a visible date.
  • Stale: the page was previously verified but needs a seasonal or regulatory refresh.

Source Policy

Regulation, permit, access, safety, and closure information should link to official government, park, resort, conservation, or operator sources. Community tips can improve planning context, but they do not replace official rules.

Corrections

If something is wrong, outdated, unsafe, or too vague, send the page URL and correction details to hello@outdoorintel.ca. Corrections should be reviewed before the page is marked verified.

Affiliate Disclosure

Outdoor Intel may earn from affiliate links. Affiliate relationships should not determine regulation advice, safety guidance, destination rankings, or verification labels.
