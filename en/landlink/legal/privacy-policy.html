Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Governing legislation: This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (S.C. 2000, c. 5) (“PIPEDA”), Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR c P-39.1) (“Québec Privacy Act”), Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (SA 2003, c P-6.5) (“AB PIPA”), and British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (SBC 2003, c 63) (“BC PIPA”), as applicable.

1. Who We Are

LandLink is a service operated by OutdoorIntel (“we,” “us,” “our”). We are the data controller responsible for the personal information collected through the Platform at landlink.outdoorintel.ca.

Privacy Officer: legal@outdoorintel.ca

3. Purposes for Collection, Use & Disclosure

Under PIPEDA Principle 2 (Identifying Purposes), we identify the following purposes at or before the time of collection:

  1. Account creation and authentication — to create your account, verify your identity, and enable secure sign-in.
  2. Verification and trust — to review PAL documents and insurance certificates, confirm validity, and display verified status to other Users.
  3. Marketplace operation — to display listings, process Hunt Requests, generate Access Agreements, and facilitate check-in/check-out.
  4. Payments — to process transactions between Hunters and Landowners and to collect the Trust & Safety Fee.
  5. Communication — to send transactional emails (booking confirmations, agreement delivery, status updates, review prompts) and to respond to support inquiries.
  6. Safety and enforcement — to investigate reports, enforce our Terms of Service, detect fraud, and suspend or terminate accounts that violate our policies.
  7. Legal compliance — to comply with applicable legal obligations, including responding to lawful requests from law enforcement or regulatory authorities.
  8. Analytics and improvement — to understand how Users interact with the Platform and to improve features, performance, and security.

We will not use or disclose personal information for purposes other than those identified above without obtaining your consent, except where permitted or required by law.

4. Consent

4.1. Express consent. We obtain your express consent when you create an account, upload verification documents, and submit a Hunt Request. By performing these actions, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information for the purposes identified in Section 3.

4.2. Implied consent. Your continued use of the Platform after being notified of changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes implied consent to the updated practices.

4.3. Withdrawal of consent. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at legal@outdoorintel.ca or by deleting your account. Withdrawal of consent may result in our inability to provide some or all Platform services to you. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

4.4. Québec residents: In accordance with the Québec Privacy Act, we collect your express consent before collecting, using, or disclosing sensitive personal information, including biometric data and government-issued identification numbers (e.g., PAL numbers).

5. Limiting Collection (PIPEDA Principle 4)

We limit the collection of personal information to what is necessary for the identified purposes. We do not collect:

  • Social Insurance Numbers (SIN)
  • Health information (unless voluntarily disclosed in a profile bio)
  • Biometric data
  • Information about children under 18 — the Platform is not intended for minors

7. Cross-Border Transfers

7.1. Our primary database is hosted on Supabase infrastructure. While we make reasonable efforts to store data in Canadian data centres, some service providers (Vercel, Resend) may process data in the United States or other jurisdictions.

7.2. Where personal information is transferred outside Canada, it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction and may be accessible to law enforcement and national security authorities of that jurisdiction. We ensure that contractual protections are in place with all service providers requiring them to safeguard personal information to a standard comparable to PIPEDA.

7.3. Québec residents: Before transferring your personal information outside Québec, we conduct a privacy impact assessment as required by the Québec Privacy Act to ensure the information will receive equivalent protection.

8. Safeguards (PIPEDA Principle 7)

We protect personal information using safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including:

  • Technical: Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest; Row-Level Security (RLS) on all database tables ensuring users can only access their own data; secure file storage for uploaded documents (private Supabase Storage bucket); magic-link authentication (no passwords stored).
  • Organizational: Access to the admin dashboard and service-role keys is restricted to authorized personnel; service providers are bound by data processing agreements; regular review of access controls.
  • Verification documents: PAL images and insurance certificates are stored in a private storage bucket accessible only to the uploading User and LandLink administrators. These documents are never displayed publicly on the Platform.

9. Retention (PIPEDA Principle 5)

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Active accounts: Retained for the life of the account.
  • Deleted accounts: Profile data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion request. Booking records and Access Agreements are retained for 7 years to comply with tax and legal record-keeping obligations.
  • Verification documents (PAL, insurance): Deleted within 90 days of account deletion or document replacement.
  • Reviews: Retained indefinitely in anonymized form after account deletion (reviewer name replaced with “Deleted User”).
  • Technical logs: Retained for 12 months, then purged.

10. Your Rights (PIPEDA Principle 9 — Individual Access)

Under PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy legislation, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by LandLink;
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
  • Withdraw consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information (subject to legal and contractual restrictions);
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to retention obligations described in Section 9);
  • Request information about our policies and practices regarding the management of personal information;
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the applicable provincial privacy commissioner.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at legal@outdoorintel.ca. We will respond within 30 days of receipt, as required by PIPEDA.

Québec residents: You also have the right to data portability and the right to be informed of automated decision-making, as required by the Québec Privacy Act.

11. Cookies & Tracking

11.1. The Platform uses essential cookies for authentication (Supabase session token) and language preference. These cookies are strictly necessary for the operation of the Platform and do not require consent.

11.2. We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If we introduce analytics or advertising cookies in the future, we will update this policy and obtain your consent where required.

12. Privacy Breach Protocol

In the event of a breach of security safeguards involving personal information that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will:

  • Report the breach to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as soon as feasible;
  • Notify affected individuals as soon as feasible, describing the nature of the breach, the information involved, steps taken to mitigate harm, and steps individuals can take to protect themselves;
  • Notify the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec if Québec residents are affected;
  • Maintain a record of all breaches for a minimum of 24 months, as required by PIPEDA.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email and by posting a notice on the Platform at least 30 days before taking effect. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
