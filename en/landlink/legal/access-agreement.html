  • Vehicle access: [vehicle_access]

[house_rules]

4. Payment

4.1. The Hunter agrees to pay the Landowner the total amount of $[total_cents / 100] CAD for the Access Period.

4.2. Payment is processed through the LandLink Platform. The Landowner receives the listed price less the LandLink Trust & Safety Fee (10%, minimum $15.00 CAD).

4.3. Cancellation and refund terms are governed by the LandLink Cancellation & Dispute Resolution Policy.

5. Hunter’s Obligations

The Hunter agrees to:

  1. Hold a valid Possession and Acquisition Licence (PAL) at all times during the Access Period;
  2. Hold all required provincial hunting licences, tags, stamps, and zone-specific permits for the species and location;
  3. Maintain active third-party liability insurance with a minimum coverage of $1,000,000 CAD;
  4. Comply with all applicable federal, provincial, territorial, and municipal laws, including the Firearms Act, the Criminal Code, the Migratory Birds Convention Act, and the applicable provincial wildlife act;
  5. Remain within the Parcel boundaries as shown on the LandLink map;
  6. Check in via the Platform upon arrival and check out upon departure;
  7. Not bring additional persons beyond the approved party size;
  8. Not sub-licence, assign, or transfer this access right;
  9. Remove all refuse, spent casings, and personal belongings;
  10. Not damage property, fences, gates, crops, livestock, structures, or natural features;
  11. Not consume alcohol or cannabis to the point of impairment while handling firearms;
  12. Report any incidents, injuries, or property damage to the Landowner and LandLink promptly;
  13. Comply with all house rules set forth in Section 3.

6. Landowner’s Obligations

The Landowner agrees to:

  1. Provide accurate information about the Parcel, including boundaries, known hazards, and access conditions;
  2. Disclose all known hazards on the Parcel, including but not limited to: active livestock, trapping devices, electric fencing, pesticide applications, unstable terrain, water hazards, and active logging or mining;
  3. Not revoke access during the Access Period without reasonable cause (safety concerns, weather emergencies, breach of Agreement by Hunter);
  4. Maintain the Parcel in a condition consistent with its described state at the time of listing;
  5. Hold legal title to the Parcel or written authorization from the legal owner to grant access;
  6. Ensure that no conflicting access has been granted that would exceed the maximum hunter limit during the Access Period.

7. Assumption of Risk

7.1. The Hunter acknowledges that hunting is an inherently dangerous activity involving firearms, wildlife, unimproved terrain, remote locations, and other hazards. The Hunter has read the LandLink Liability & Assumption of Risk Policy and voluntarily assumes all risks, known and unknown, associated with entering the Parcel and engaging in hunting activities.

7.2. The Hunter acknowledges that the Landowner has not made any representations or warranties regarding the safety or suitability of the Parcel beyond what is stated in the LandLink listing and this Agreement.

8. Indemnification

8.1. Hunter indemnifies Landowner: The Hunter agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Landowner from any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising out of or related to the Hunter’s acts or omissions on the Parcel, including personal injury to the Hunter or members of the Hunter’s party, damage to the Parcel, and violations of law.

8.2. Landowner indemnifies Hunter: The Landowner agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Hunter from any and all claims arising from the Landowner’s failure to disclose known hazards or material misrepresentation of the Parcel in the listing.

8.3. Both parties indemnify LandLink: Both the Hunter and Landowner agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless LandLink, OutdoorIntel, and their respective officers, directors, employees, and agents from any claims arising from this Agreement or any activity on the Parcel.

9. Limitation of Liability

9.1. TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

9.2. The Landowner’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the amount paid by the Hunter for the Access Period.

9.3. Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits liability for death or personal injury caused by gross negligence or wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be excluded by law.

10. Revocation & Early Termination

10.1. The Landowner may revoke access immediately if the Hunter: (a) breaches any term of this Agreement; (b) poses a safety risk; (c) engages in illegal activity; or (d) is found to be under the influence of alcohol or drugs while handling firearms.

10.2. The Hunter may terminate this Agreement early by checking out via the Platform and departing the Parcel.

10.3. Refund entitlement upon early termination is governed by the LandLink Cancellation & Dispute Resolution Policy.

12. Dispute Resolution

12.1. Disputes arising under this Agreement shall first be submitted to the LandLink dispute resolution process as described in the Cancellation & Dispute Resolution Policy.

12.2. If unresolved after mediation, disputes shall be submitted to binding arbitration in the province where the Parcel is located, in accordance with that province’s arbitration legislation.

12.3. Nothing in this Section prevents either party from seeking injunctive relief in a court of competent jurisdiction to prevent irreparable harm.

13. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the province in which the Parcel is located and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein.

14. General Provisions

14.1. Entire agreement. This Agreement, together with the LandLink Terms of Service and referenced policies, constitutes the entire agreement between the Landowner and Hunter regarding the specified Access Period.

14.2. Severability. If any provision is held invalid, the remainder continues in full force.

14.3. No waiver. Failure to enforce any provision does not waive the right to enforce it later.

14.4. Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.

14.5. Language. This Agreement is drafted in English. In the event of a conflict with any translation, the English version prevails.

14.6. Survival. Sections 7 (Assumption of Risk), 8 (Indemnification), 9 (Limitation of Liability), and 12 (Dispute Resolution) survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.
