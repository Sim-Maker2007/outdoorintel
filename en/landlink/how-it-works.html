How LandLink works

Two sides, one trust layer. Landowners list parcels; hunters apply with verified credentials; LandLink handles the agreement and insurance.

For landowners

  1. Draw your parcel on a satellite map. Set species, weapons, max hunters, vehicle access, dates, and price (or mark it free).
  2. We review the listing within 24 hours and confirm it complies with your province\u2019s rules.
  3. Hunters apply \u2014 you see their verified PAL status, insurance, reviews, and a message.
  4. Approve or decline from your dashboard. Approved requests auto-generate an access agreement.
  5. Hunters check in on arrival via a shared link. You always know who\u2019s on your land.
  6. Review them after. Good hunters build reputations; problems get flagged.

For hunters

  1. Create your profile. Upload your PAL and proof of liability insurance. We verify within 24h.
  2. Browse parcels filtered by species, weapon, province, dates, and price.
  3. Apply with your dates, party size, and a short message about yourself.
  4. Sign the access agreement once the landowner approves.
  5. Hunt. Check in on arrival, check out on departure.
  6. Leave a review. Build your reputation on the platform.

Responsible for your own compliance

Every hunter is responsible for holding the correct provincial licence, tags, and zone-specific permits. LandLink surfaces regulator links on every parcel, but we don\u2019t replace your obligation to know the rules for where and when you\u2019re hunting.
