Trust & Safety

The platform is only worth using if both sides feel safe. Here\u2019s how we protect landowners and hunters.

🛡️

PAL verification

Every hunter uploads their Possession and Acquisition Licence. We review it before they can send a request.

💼

Mandatory liability insurance

Hunters must have active liability coverage. We collect proof and block expired policies from booking.

📜

Counsel-reviewed agreement

Every approved booking generates a written access agreement with indemnity, waivers, and provincial rider \u2014 reviewed by Canadian hunting/agricultural counsel.

⏱️

Check-in / check-out

Shared short-links log exactly when hunters arrive and leave the property. No surprises.

⭐

Two-way reviews

Both parties rate each other after every hunt. Patterns of bad behaviour surface quickly; good actors build trust.

⛔

Kill-switch

Admins can suspend a parcel or user immediately if something goes wrong. You can also report a user from any page.
