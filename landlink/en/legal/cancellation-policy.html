Cancellation & Dispute Resolution Policy

Effective Date: April 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Part A — Cancellation & Refunds

1. Cancellation by Hunter

1.1. Hunters may cancel an approved Hunt Request through their dashboard. The refund amount depends on when the cancellation is made relative to the Access Period start date (date_from):

Cancellation Window Refund to Hunter Landowner Receives
14+ days before start date 100% refund (less Trust & Safety Fee) $0
7–13 days before start date 50% refund (less Trust & Safety Fee) 50% of listed price
Less than 7 days before start date No refund 100% of listed price
After check-in (early departure) No refund 100% of listed price

1.2. The LandLink Trust & Safety Fee (10%, minimum $15.00 CAD) is non-refundable in all cases, as it covers verification, agreement generation, and platform costs already incurred.

1.3. Cancellations are effective immediately upon submission. The Hunt Request status changes to “cancelled.”

2. Cancellation by Landowner

2.1. Landowners may cancel an approved Hunt Request through their dashboard. Because Hunters rely on confirmed bookings to plan travel and take time off work, Landowner cancellations carry the following consequences:

Cancellation Window Hunter Receives Consequence to Landowner
14+ days before start date Full refund including Trust & Safety Fee Warning recorded on account
Less than 14 days before start date Full refund including Trust & Safety Fee Warning recorded; listing temporarily paused for review
After check-in (mid-hunt revocation) Full refund including Trust & Safety Fee Account review; possible suspension

2.2. Exception — justified revocation: A Landowner may revoke access at any time without penalty if the Hunter: (a) breaches the Access Agreement; (b) poses a safety risk; (c) is under the influence of alcohol or drugs while handling firearms; (d) engages in illegal activity; or (e) exceeds the approved party size or boundaries. In such cases, no refund is owed to the Hunter.

2.3. Repeated Landowner cancellations (3 or more in a 12-month period) without justified cause may result in permanent listing removal and account suspension.

3. Cancellation by LandLink

3.1. LandLink reserves the right to cancel any booking at any time if we reasonably believe that:

  • A safety risk exists for either party;
  • The booking violates applicable law or our Terms of Service;
  • Fraud or misrepresentation is suspected;
  • A government-issued fire ban, hunting season closure, or emergency order affects the Parcel;
  • Either party’s verification documents (PAL, insurance) have expired or been invalidated.

3.2. In LandLink-initiated cancellations: the Hunter receives a full refund including the Trust & Safety Fee. If the cause is attributable to the Landowner, the Landowner may be charged a cancellation fee. If attributable to the Hunter, no refund is issued.

4. Force Majeure

4.1. If a booking cannot proceed due to events beyond either party’s reasonable control (natural disasters, government orders, fire bans, severe weather making access unsafe, pandemic restrictions), the Hunter receives a full refund including the Trust & Safety Fee, and no penalty is applied to either party.

4.2. LandLink determines, at its reasonable discretion, whether a force majeure event applies.

5. Refund Processing

5.1. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.

5.2. LandLink is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment processors.

Part B — Dispute Resolution

6. Scope

This dispute resolution process applies to disputes between Users (Landowner vs. Hunter) and disputes between a User and LandLink, arising from or related to the Platform, a booking, or an Access Agreement.

7. Step 1 — Direct Resolution (Required)

7.1. Before involving LandLink, both parties must attempt to resolve the dispute directly by communicating through the Platform messaging system or by email.

7.2. Both parties must make a good-faith effort to resolve the matter within 14 days of the dispute arising.

8. Step 2 — LandLink Mediation

8.1. If direct resolution fails, either party may submit a Dispute Report to LandLink at disputes@outdoorintel.ca. The report must include:

  • The Hunt Request ID;
  • A factual description of the dispute;
  • Supporting evidence (photos, screenshots, correspondence);
  • The outcome sought.

8.2. LandLink will acknowledge the Dispute Report within 3 business days and assign a case reviewer.

8.3. The case reviewer will gather information from both parties, review the booking records, Access Agreement, check-in/check-out logs, and any available evidence.

8.4. LandLink will issue a mediation decision within 15 business days of receiving the Dispute Report. This decision may include:

  • Full or partial refund to the Hunter;
  • Payment release to the Landowner;
  • Account warnings, suspensions, or terminations;
  • Listing removal or modification;
  • Referral to law enforcement or wildlife authorities (if illegal activity is suspected).

8.5. LandLink’s mediation decision is non-binding. Either party may proceed to formal dispute resolution if unsatisfied.

9. Step 3 — Formal Dispute Resolution

9.1. Disputes between Users (Landowner vs. Hunter): If LandLink mediation does not resolve the dispute, the parties agree to submit the dispute to binding arbitration conducted by a single arbitrator in the province where the Parcel is located, in accordance with that province’s arbitration legislation (e.g., Arbitration Act, 1991, S.O. 1991, c. 17 in Ontario). The arbitrator’s decision is final and binding.

9.2. Disputes between a User and LandLink: If LandLink mediation does not resolve the dispute, the dispute shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the Province of Ontario, sitting in Ottawa, as set out in the Terms of Service.

9.3. Small claims exception: Either party may bring an individual action in small claims court in the province where the Parcel is located (or, for disputes with LandLink, in Ontario) if the claim falls within the court’s monetary jurisdiction.

9.4. No class actions. To the maximum extent permitted by law, you agree that any dispute will be resolved on an individual basis. You waive any right to participate in a class action, collective action, or representative proceeding against LandLink.

10. Reporting Illegal Activity

10.1. If you witness or suspect illegal activity during a hunt (poaching, trespassing, unsafe firearm use, impaired operation of firearms), you should:

  1. Ensure your immediate safety;
  2. Contact local emergency services (911) if there is an immediate threat;
  3. Contact the provincial conservation officer tip line (available via the regulator links on each Parcel page);
  4. Report the incident to LandLink at safety@outdoorintel.ca.

10.2. LandLink will cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations and may disclose User information as required by law or court order, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

11. Property Damage Claims

11.1. If a Landowner alleges property damage caused by a Hunter, the Landowner must:

  1. Document the damage with photographs and a written description within 48 hours of the Hunter’s check-out;
  2. Submit a Dispute Report to LandLink with the documentation and an estimate of repair costs;
  3. Allow the Hunter 7 days to respond to the claim.

11.2. LandLink may, at its discretion, withhold the Hunter’s payout (if any) pending resolution of a property damage claim, for a maximum of 30 days.

11.3. If the parties cannot agree, the matter proceeds through the dispute resolution steps above. Property damage claims exceeding $5,000 CAD are beyond LandLink’s mediation scope and should be pursued through insurance or the courts.

12. Limitation Period

12.1. Dispute Reports must be submitted within 30 days of the event giving rise to the dispute (or within 30 days of discovering the issue, if it was not immediately apparent).

12.2. This internal deadline does not affect statutory limitation periods under applicable provincial law.
