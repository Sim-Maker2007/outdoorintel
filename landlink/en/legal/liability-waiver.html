Liability & Assumption of Risk

Effective Date: April 6, 2026  |  Last Updated: April 6, 2026

IMPORTANT: This document contains a waiver of certain legal rights. Please read it carefully. If you do not understand any part of this document, seek independent legal counsel before using the Platform or participating in any hunt booked through LandLink.

1. Acknowledgement of Inherent Risks

1.1. Hunting is an inherently dangerous activity. By using the LandLink Platform to book or provide hunting access, you acknowledge and accept that hunting involves serious risks that cannot be eliminated regardless of the care taken. These inherent risks include, but are not limited to:

  • Firearms-related risks: Accidental discharge, ricochet, misfires, mechanical failure of firearms or ammunition, and injury or death caused by the use or misuse of firearms, crossbows, archery equipment, or muzzleloaders;
  • Wildlife-related risks: Encounters with aggressive, wounded, or unpredictable wildlife, including large game (moose, elk, bear), predators (wolves, coyotes), and venomous or disease-carrying animals;
  • Environmental risks: Rough, uneven, or unstable terrain; fallen trees; concealed holes or ditches; water hazards (rivers, marshes, beaver dams, ice); extreme weather (hypothermia, heatstroke, lightning, blizzards); wildfire; avalanche; and limited cellular or emergency service coverage;
  • Agricultural and rural hazards: Active livestock operations, electric fencing, pesticide or herbicide residues, farm machinery, grain bins, irrigation equipment, and trapping devices;
  • Transportation risks: Navigating remote access roads, ATV use, crossing waterways, and limited roadside assistance;
  • Medical emergency risks: Remote locations may be far from hospitals or emergency medical services, potentially delaying treatment of injuries;
  • Third-party risks: Actions or negligence of other hunters, Landowners, neighbouring property users, or trespassers.

1.2. You further acknowledge that the above list is not exhaustive and that other unknown or unanticipated risks may exist. You voluntarily assume all such risks, whether known or unknown, foreseeable or unforeseeable.

2. Voluntary Assumption of Risk

2.1. By creating a LandLink account and using the Platform to book hunting access or to grant hunting access on your property, you freely, voluntarily, and without duress assume all risks associated with hunting activities, property access, and all related activities, whether or not described above.

2.2. This assumption of risk applies to personal injury, death, property damage, and financial loss.

2.3. You confirm that your participation in hunting activities booked through the Platform is entirely voluntary and that you have chosen to participate with full knowledge and appreciation of the inherent dangers involved.

4.2. This indemnification obligation survives the termination or expiration of your account and any Access Agreement.

5. Landowner-Specific Provisions

5.1. Occupiers’ liability. If you are a Landowner, you acknowledge that allowing hunters onto your property may engage your duties under your province’s occupiers’ liability legislation, including but not limited to:

  • Occupiers’ Liability Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. O.2 (Ontario)
  • Occupiers Liability Act, R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 337 (British Columbia)
  • Occupiers’ Liability Act, R.S.A. 2000, c. O-4 (Alberta)
  • Occupiers’ Liability Act, C.C.S.M. c. O8 (Manitoba)
  • Occupiers’ Liability Act, R.S.N.S. 1996, c. 27 (Nova Scotia)
  • Occupiers’ Liability Act, S.P.E.I. 2002, c. O-2 (PEI)
  • And analogous legislation in other provinces and territories, or the Civil Code of Québec (arts. 1457, 1465, 1467) for Québec.

5.2. Many provinces distinguish between duties owed to persons entering for recreational purposes (often a lower standard of care) and other entrants. However, charging a fee for access may affect which standard of care applies. You are strongly advised to consult a lawyer in your province to understand your specific obligations.

5.3. The Access Agreement includes an assumption-of-risk and indemnification clause signed by the Hunter. While this provides significant legal protection, it may not fully override your statutory duties under occupiers’ liability law. Maintaining adequate liability insurance is essential.

6. Hunter-Specific Provisions

6.1. Fitness and competence. By submitting a Hunt Request, you represent that you are physically and mentally fit to participate in hunting activities, that you are competent in the safe handling of the weapon type you have selected, and that you have completed any hunter education programs required by your province.

6.2. Equipment responsibility. You are solely responsible for the condition, legality, and safe use of all equipment you bring onto a Parcel, including firearms, ammunition, tree stands, blinds, decoys, and vehicles.

6.3. Emergency preparedness. You acknowledge that many Parcels are in remote or rural areas with limited cellular service and emergency medical access. You are responsible for carrying appropriate first-aid supplies, communication devices, and informing a third party of your hunting plans and expected return time.

6.4. Firearm transportation. You are solely responsible for the legal transportation of firearms to and from the Parcel in compliance with the Firearms Act (S.C. 1995, c. 39), including requirements for locking devices, secure storage, and authorization to transport (ATT) where applicable.

7. Insurance Requirements

7.1. Hunters must maintain third-party liability insurance with a minimum coverage of $1,000,000 CAD. This coverage must be effective for the entire duration of any booking.

7.2. Landowners are strongly encouraged to maintain farm/ranch liability insurance or a commercial general liability policy that covers recreational third-party access to their property.

7.3. LandLink does not provide, underwrite, or guarantee any insurance coverage. The Platform’s verification of insurance documents is an administrative check only and does not constitute an endorsement of the adequacy, validity, or scope of any policy.

7.4. You are responsible for understanding your insurance coverage, its exclusions, and its limitations. Many standard homeowner or hunting insurance policies contain exclusions for commercial or fee-based access arrangements. Confirm with your insurer that your policy covers activities booked through LandLink.

8. No Guarantee of Safety

8.1. LandLink’s verification processes (PAL review, insurance review, listing review, admin kill-switch, check-in/check-out system, and two-way reviews) are designed to promote trust and accountability. They do not guarantee safety.

8.2. No technology platform can eliminate the inherent risks of hunting, rural property access, or human behaviour. You must exercise your own judgment, take your own precautions, and make your own assessment of risk before and during any hunt.

9. Provincial Limitations

9.1. Some provinces may limit or restrict the enforceability of waivers, releases, or assumption-of-risk agreements, particularly in cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct. Nothing in this Policy is intended to waive, release, or limit liability to the extent prohibited by applicable law.

9.2. In Québec, liability waivers are interpreted restrictively under the Civil Code of Québec. Québec residents should be aware that certain waiver provisions may be limited or inapplicable under Québec civil law.

9.3. If any provision of this Policy is found to be unenforceable in a particular jurisdiction, it shall be severed only to the extent necessary, and all remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
