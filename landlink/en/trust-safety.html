Trust & Safety

The platform is only worth using if both sides feel safe. Here’s how we protect landowners and guests across every activity on LandLink.

🛡️

Verified identity

Every guest uploads a government-issued ID. We review it before they can send a booking request.

💼

Activity-specific credentials

Hunting bookings require a valid PAL and liability insurance. Dock & boat bookings ask for a Pleasure Craft Operator Card. Ranges require liability coverage. We enforce it before approval.

📜

Counsel-reviewed agreement

Every approved booking generates a written access agreement with indemnity, waivers, and a per-activity provincial rider — reviewed by Canadian counsel.

⏱️

Check-in / check-out

Shared short-links log exactly when guests arrive and leave the property. Landowners always know who’s on their land.

⭐

Two-way reviews

Both parties rate each other after every booking. Patterns of bad behaviour surface quickly; good actors build trust.

⛔

Kill-switch

Admins can suspend a parcel, listing, or user immediately if something goes wrong. You can also report from any page.
