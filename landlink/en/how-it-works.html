How LandLink works

One marketplace for every use of private outdoor land. Landowners list parcels; guests book the specific activity they need; LandLink handles discovery, agreements, payments, and trust.

One parcel, many uses

A 200-acre woodlot is not just a hunting lease. It’s hunting in October, snowmobile trails in January, sugar shack tours in March, ATV access in July, and primitive camping every weekend in between. LandLink lets one parcel become a year-round asset instead of an eight-month-dead one — which is exactly why most outdoor marketplaces die.

For landowners

  1. Draw your parcel on a satellite map. Tell us what’s on the land — lake, forest, trails, cabin, dock, sugar shack, whatever applies.
  2. Pick what you’ll host. Hunting, camping, fishing, ATV, snowmobile, sugar shack, dock access, foraging, dog training, photography, wellness retreats — pick any combination. Each one becomes its own listing with its own price and rules.
  3. We review within 24h and confirm your listing complies with provincial regulations.
  4. Guests request specific bookings. You see verified ID, relevant credentials (PAL for hunting, insurance where it’s required), reviews from previous hosts, and a message.
  5. Approve or decline from your dashboard. Approved bookings auto-generate a counsel-reviewed access agreement signed by both parties.
  6. Get paid. LandLink takes 15% host-side on paid bookings. Payouts after check-out.
  7. Review the guest after. Good guests build reputations that open doors; problems get flagged early.

For guests

  1. Create your profile. Upload a government ID — and any activity-specific credentials you have (PAL, hunter ed, insurance, pleasure-craft operator card, etc.). We verify within 24h.
  2. Browse by activity or location. Filter by hunting, camping, ATV, dock access, sugar shack, whatever you need.
  3. Book the specific use. If a parcel offers multiple activities, pick the one you want — deer hunting in October is a different booking from that same parcel’s sugar shack day pass in March.
  4. Sign the access agreement once the landowner approves. A 5% service fee is added at checkout.
  5. Check in on arrival via a shared link. Enjoy the land.
  6. Leave a review. Build your reputation for the next booking.

Platform economics

  • 15% host fee on paid bookings
  • 5% guest service fee at checkout
  • Free listings carry no platform fee — some owners just want pressure on deer, goose management, or goodwill
  • No listing fee. You only pay when you earn.

You’re responsible for your own compliance

Every guest is responsible for holding the correct provincial licences, tags, and activity-specific permits. Every landowner is responsible for confirming that the activities they host are legal on their property. LandLink surfaces regulator links and an activity-specific checklist on every listing, but we don’t replace your obligation to know the rules for where and when you’re operating.
