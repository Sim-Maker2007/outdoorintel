Let’s start with the basics

Give your parcel a name and tell us where it is. This is the land itself — you’ll pick what can happen on it in a later step.

Draw your parcel on the map

Outline the property boundary. Only you and admins see the exact shape — guests see a fuzzy circle until their booking is approved.

Click the polygon tool on the map, tap points to draw the boundary, close it to save.

What’s on the land?

Tick anything that applies. These features are how guests will find your parcel.

What can guests do here?

Pick every activity you’d consider hosting. One parcel can offer many — the same woodlot can host deer hunting in October, sledding in January, and camping every summer weekend.

Set your pricing

One row per selected activity. Mark free if you just want land managed (pest control, goodwill, pressure on deer, etc).

LandLink takes a 15% host fee on paid bookings and charges guests a 5% service fee. Free listings have no platform fee.

Review & submit

We’ll review your listing within 24h and email you when it goes live.

By submitting, you confirm you have the legal right to grant access on this property and that the activities you’ve selected comply with provincial regulations.
