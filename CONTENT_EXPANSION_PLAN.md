# OutdoorIntel Content Expansion Plan

## Executive Summary
Goal: Build 100+ new content pieces to augment current blog (20 posts) and directory (499 spots). Focus on SEO, affiliate revenue, and user engagement.

---

## Phase 1: Spot Detail Pages (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
**Why**: Each spot page targets local SEO, unique long-tail keywords
**Timeline**: 4-6 weeks
**Revenue**: Medium ( affiliate gear links on each spot page)

### Format Template
Each spot gets:
- 800-1200 words
- Seasonal fishing calendar
- Species present + best baits
- Access directions + parking
- Regulations summary
- Nearby amenities
- "Gear for this spot" affiliate section
- Photos (user-submitted or Creative Commons)

### Priority Spots (Top 50 First)
**Ontario**:
1. Lake Simcoe (ice fishing mecca)
2. Ottawa River (bass + walleye)
3. Lake Ontario tributaries
4. Algonquin Park lakes
5. Kawartha Lakes
6. Lake Nipissing

**Quebec**:
7. Saint Lawrence River
8. Outaouais River region
9. Lac des Deux Montagnes
10. Lake Memphremagog

**West**:
11. Lake Superior (Ontario side)
12. Lake Huron/Georgian Bay
13. Okanagan Lake (BC)

**Big-name spots first** - they'll rank fastest.
